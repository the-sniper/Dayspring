Add a high-performing LinkedIn post to the user's writing samples knowledge base.

---

## STEP 1 — GET THE POST

Ask:
"Paste the full post text you want to add as a writing sample."

Wait for the post text. Accept it exactly as pasted.

---

## STEP 2 — GET PERFORMANCE NOTE

Ask:
"Any performance note? (e.g. '2.4k impressions, 38 comments, 12 reposts') — press Enter to skip."

Accept whatever format is provided, or nothing.

---

## STEP 3 — GET CONTEXT

Ask:
"One line on why this post worked well, or what made it special — press Enter to skip."

---

## STEP 4 — APPEND TO WRITING SAMPLES

Read knowledge_base/writing_samples.md first to understand the current format.

Then append the new sample at the end in this format:

```markdown
---

## Sample — [DATE]
**Performance:** [performance note, or "Not tracked"]
**Why it works:** [context note, or "Added as reference"]

[Full post text exactly as pasted]
```

Confirm: "Added to writing_samples.md. This will be used to calibrate voice in future pipeline runs."

---

## WHEN TO USE THIS

Run /add-writing-sample after:
- A post gets unusually high engagement (impressions, comments, shares)
- You write a post manually that captures your voice particularly well
- Someone compliments a specific post and you want to remember the style
