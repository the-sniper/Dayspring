# Content Operating Rules

> Sensible defaults ship here so the pipeline works out of the box. `/setup` personalizes the user-specific parts (length, hashtags, emoji, CTA, banned words). Treat this file as law during writing and editing.

## Platform
- LinkedIn, text posts
- Professional but human — never corporate, never stiff

## The non-negotiables (apply to every post)

1. **Opinion over information.** Anyone can summarize. The differentiator is a clear, held view on what the information *means*. Every post needs a take, not a recap.
2. **Voice mode — A or C, never B:**
   - **Mode A — personal anchor:** a real, specific story. ("Last week at the business…", "When I left…")
   - **Mode C — point of view:** your read on a trend/idea. ("I think…", "my read on this is…", "what I'm seeing…")
   - **Mode B — faceless article:** facts with no author fingerprint. **Never.** Test: remove the name — could you tell who wrote it? If no, rewrite.
3. **Specific over generic.** Real stories, real numbers, real names beat hypotheticals every time.

## Post structure
1. **Hook** (line 1–2): earns the scroll-stop. Bold claim / surprising number / provocative question / specific moment.
   → Then ONE blank line, pushing the rest below the fold.
2. **Body:** story, data, or framework. Short paragraphs, 1–2 sentences each.
3. **Insight:** the "so what" — one clean opinion, not a restatement.
4. **CTA:** one action. Usually a question to drive comments.

## Formatting
- Short paragraphs. White space is not wasted space.
- Arrow lists (→) for takeaways. Numbered lists only for real sequences.
- Zero buzzwords: "leverage", "synergy", "impactful", "passionate", "excited to share".

## User preferences (set by /setup)
- **Length:** medium (150–250 words). Never over 350.
- **Hashtags:** 1–2 max, at the very end.
- **Emoji:** sparingly.
- **CTA style:** end with a question.
- **Banned words / topics:** _add yours via /setup._

## Quality checks (run before presenting)
- Clear opinion someone could disagree with?
- Mode A or C — not B?
- Hook earns the scroll-stop?
- Any line that sounds like AI or corporate speak — rewritten?
- Any sentence that could be cut without loss — cut it.
