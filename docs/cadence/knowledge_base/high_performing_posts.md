# High-Performing Post Patterns — Structure Reference

> These are structural reference patterns, not content to copy. Study the shape — hook, flow, ending. Every post you publish must be original and anchored to *your* real experience.
>
> Swap these for your own favourite high-performers anytime (Knowledge tab or by editing this file). The more the system sees what *you* think great looks like, the better it gets.

## Pattern 1 — Raw numbers hook
Open with a stark result or milestone (numbers, no fluff). Subvert the expected "10 things I did" follow-up. End on a simple, honest truth. No CTA needed — authenticity carries it.
**Use for:** milestones, transitions, results.

## Pattern 2 — Stat contrast → arrow list → punchy insight
Hook on a surprising contrast ("300k views, 437 applicants, hired 1"). Specific failure example. Scannable arrow list of what worked. Close with a sharp one-line insight. Lowercase = conversational.
**Use for:** lessons, how-tos, behind-the-scenes.

## Pattern 3 — Third-person discovered story
"I met someone who…" / "found a founder doing X." Their exact process as a short list. Why it works. Results. The third-person frame makes the insight feel discovered, not lectured.
**Use for:** frameworks, business models, trends.

## Pattern 4 — Provocative one-liner → specific story → honest caveat → question
Bold opener stated plainly, no hedging. A specific, named, located story. An honest limit ("this won't fix everything, but…"). End on a direct question.
**Use for:** opinions, contrarian takes.

## Pattern 5 — Week-by-week breakdown → analogy → consistency thesis
Relatable failure pattern first ("Week 1: 500 views. Week 4: they quit."). Reframe with an analogy (compound interest). Close: the winners aren't the most talented, they're the most consistent. Question CTA.
**Use for:** journey posts, consistency themes.

## Pattern 6 — Discovered insight → market trend → reframe
Open with a "secret" that creates tension (low followers, high revenue). Zoom out to the trend it signals. End by redefining what the thing actually is.
**Use for:** AI, business models, where-things-are-going takes.

## Format rules observed across all of them
- Short paragraphs, 1–2 sentences max.
- Arrow lists (→) for scannable takeaways.
- Lowercase in the body = conversational, not corporate.
- A question at the end generates comments.
- 0–2 hashtags, never stuffed.
- 150–300 words is the sweet spot.
